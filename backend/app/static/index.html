Scene Sense

Ask about any scene.

Describe a moment from a movie, show, or video — get the scene found, explained, and clipped, with sources.

About

Finding the exact moment.

Scene Sense is a conversational clip finder. Describe a moment from a film, show, or video — it identifies the source, fetches and transcribes the footage, pinpoints the moment down to the spoken line, cuts a playable clip, and explains the scene with cited sources.

Why it was made

Because half-remembering a scene usually means scrubbing through a whole video hoping to spot it. That felt like a search problem, not a patience problem — so this tool treats it as one: transcript search with word-level timestamps, a semantic matcher, and an emotion signal to break ties.

How it works

  • A local LLM orchestrates the tools — identify, fetch, locate, clip.
  • Whisper transcribes on-device with word-level timestamps.
  • Semantic search over transcript windows finds the moment; a quoted line is pinned to the word via fuzzy matching on the word stream.
  • Facial-expression analysis (emotion only, never identity) nudges ambiguous matches.
  • ffmpeg cuts a standalone clip; the answer cites its sources.

Runs locally on your machine. Videos are fetched for personal reference; nothing you ask leaves your device except the video search itself.

Guide

Asking well.

Scene Sense finds what you can describe. The more anchors you give it — title, a quoted line, who's in the scene, the feeling of it — the sharper the match.

What works

  • Name the work. “…in The Dark Knight” beats “…in that Batman movie”.
  • Quote what you remember, in quotes. A quoted line is matched word-by-word against the transcript, so the clip lands exactly on it.
  • One scene per question. Every question is resolved fresh, from scratch — ask about a new scene any time, no reset needed.
  • Describe the emotion. Words like furious, celebrates, heartbroken feed the emotion timeline and help break ties between similar dialogue.
  • Verify quotes with “did they really say…” — you'll get the exact line, its source, and a confidence instead of a clip.

Try one

Power use

  • Load your own video with POST /api/load_url or /api/upload — it stays pinned to your session, and every question searches that video until you replace it.
  • Clips land in backend/clips/ as standalone mp4s — keep them, share them.
